# Story continuity

## Chapter 2 decisions — owner accepted 2026-11-19

- Skitters hatches, calls Shir “Mama”, accidentally sneezes a small flame; Shir is frightened, her reaction frightens him, and he camouflages against leaves. He is not deliberately attacking or confidently controlling fire.
- Camouflage changes scale colour to blend with surroundings. He remains physically present and the same size/anatomy; he does not teleport, shrink, become a ghost or disappear from reality. Normal colour is light turquoise/blue-green. Fear is the initial trigger; calming reveals normal colour again. Eyes/tail provide discovery clues.
- Future purpose: help explain why passersby/classmates may not notice him when he follows Shir to school. Do not establish perfect invisibility, silence, invisible footprints/shadows, invisible carried objects, voluntary mastery or permanent concealment without future story discussion. His voice, movement and sneezes still create tension.
- Shir looks around and calls for someone; no response. She does NOT know he is abandoned, orphaned or alone forever. She believes a caretaker may return and urges him to wait beside his egg. The caretaker's identity/existence/whereabouts remain unresolved.
- Skitters follows despite her instructions to stay. She does not decide to take someone else's hatchling or carry him away. Both leave the garden at chapter end.
- Do not discuss/explain the bell in Chapter 2. No confirmed birthday mechanism, ownership prophecy or biological maternity. “Mama” is his attachment to Shir, not factual parentage.
- Preserve two gentle jokes: “I am nine! I am not your mama”, then Shir ducks at an expected sneeze but gets a burp. Ending is sandal contact, “Mama”, “Oh, no.” School lateness plus fear/compassion remain believable child emotions.
- Skitters is his production name; naming him in reader text is not yet written. Later naming scene can arise from his quick little feet.
- Hatchling stage remains fixed throughout Chapter 2 and subsequent scenes until explicit growth is agreed. Never age/grow him merely to satisfy a pose. Camouflage changes colour, not scale.
