# Chapter 1: The Little Bell

Status: text approved by owner on 2026-11-18. 16 pages. Art production authorized. Deploy to staging for review; publish in the main book only after explicit owner approval of the staged version.

## Page 1

Today is my birthday.  
I am nine!  
And today, I can walk to school  
all by myself.

## Page 2

Dad stands by the door.  
“Happy birthday, Shir!” he says.  
“Good luck today!”  
I smile. “Bye, Dad!”

## Page 3

My pink bag is on my back.  
I know the way to school.  
Past the crosswalk. Past the tall buildings.  
I walk this way every day.

## Page 4

But today, something is different.

*Ding!*

A tiny bell.

## Page 5

I stop and look around.  
A car goes past.  
A woman walks her dog.  
Where is the bell?

## Page 6

Then I see it.  
An old wooden door  
in the wall of a building.

That door was not here yesterday.

## Page 7

People walk past the door.  
No one looks at it.  
But I cannot look away.

Where did it come from?

## Page 8

I take one step closer.

*Ding!*

I jump.  
There it is again!

## Page 9

I put my hand on the handle.  
I push.  
I pull.

The door will not open.

## Page 10

“Well… happy birthday to me,” I say.

*Click.*

I am still holding the handle.  
But I did not turn it.

## Page 11

The door opens a little.

*DING!*

The bell is louder now.  
It is on the other side.

## Page 12

I push the door wide.

A garden?

Here?  
Between all these buildings?

## Page 13

I should go to school.  
I do not want to be late.  
I am not allowed to go in here.  
What if it is not safe?

Just one look…  
I step inside.

## Page 14

Soft, golden light fills the garden.  
It shines on the green leaves.  
There is no sound from the street.

Even the air feels different.

## Page 15

I walk past the green bushes.

*Click. Click.*

I stop.

*Click. Click.*

Something is behind that bush.

## Page 16

I look over the bush.

An egg.  
An egg as big as my school bag!

Then the egg moves.

## Editorial and future art notes (not reader text)

- Dad's face need not be shown in the doorway farewell.
- Shir's bag is mostly pink with a KPop Demon Hunters print featuring the three HUNTR/X girls. This supersedes the yellow backpack in existing artwork. Update the canonical Shir sheet and cover only after finishing copy, per the owner's sequencing instruction. No new art generated for this draft.
- Ordinary modern Israeli neighborhood, tall buildings, modern wall; old wooden door matches the cover. Other people do not notice it. This is special for Shir, but do not establish whether others cannot see it.
- The reason the door appears/opens for Shir is unresolved. Birthday versus another connection is deliberately unknown; the birthday joke is not confirmation of a password or a ninth-birthday rule.
- Keep the birthday joke. Use occasional gentle, character-driven humor in future chapters without undercutting emotional moments.
- Page 14 combines awareness of danger/rules with worry about being late. Carry believable nine-year-old emotional conflicts into later chapters: curiosity versus safety, independence versus needing help, keeping a promise versus helping a friend. Keep language concrete and simple; avoid moral lectures.
- Garden magic comes from light, glow and atmosphere, not magical beings or fantastical flowers. No creature reveal in this chapter.
- Egg height is approximately one-quarter to one-third of Shir's height. School-bag comparison is child-friendly text, not an exact scale specification.
- Final cliffhanger is exactly “Then the egg moves.” Save hatching and meeting Skitters for subsequent collaborative planning; no later plot is approved yet.
- Bell source remains an open story question; agree an answer before planting specific explanatory clues.
