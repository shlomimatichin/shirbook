← ChaptersCHAPTER 2 · I AM NOT YOUR MAMA!

Crack.
I take a step back.
A little hole opens in the egg.
Something blue-green pokes out.
A nose?

Two tiny feet push through.
Then a head.
Then two small wings.
A dragon!

He looks at me.
I look at him.
“Mama?” he says.

“Me?” I say.
“I am nine!”
“I am not your mama.”
He puts his little feet on my sandal.

“Where is your mama?” I ask.
I look behind the bush.
I look between the trees.
“Hello? Is anyone here?”

I wait.
No answer.
“Maybe someone will come back,” I say.
“You should wait by your egg.”

His little nose moves.
His eyes close.
“Are you all right?”
“Ah… ah…”

“CHOO!”
A tiny flame shoots out.
A dry leaf catches fire.
I jump back.
“STOP!”

I stamp on the leaf.
Once. Twice.
The fire is out.
My heart is going so fast.

I turn to the dragon.
But he is not there.
There is only the bush.
“Where did you go?”

A small sound comes from the leaves.
“Mama?”
Two eyes look up at me.
Then I see a green tail.

His green scales turn blue-green again.
Little by little, I can see him.
He is shaking.
“You are scared too,” I say.

I sit beside him.
“No more fire, please.”
He opens his mouth.
I duck.
A tiny burp comes out.

I stand up.
“I have to go to school.”
“Someone may come back for you.”
“Please stay here.”
I walk to the door.

Tap. Tap. Tap.
Little feet follow me.
I step out of the garden.
He steps out too.

“You cannot come to school.”
He sits with one foot on my sandal.
“Mama.”
Oh, no.
