# Skitters: growth and continuity

Production scale targets, not measurements from the compressed concept lineup. Shir's reference height is a drawing convention of 1.3 m, not a claim about her actual height.

| Stage | Shoulder height | Body length, excluding tail | Story scale |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Hatchling | 0.18 m | 0.30 m | Small enough to cradle |
| Baby | 0.40 m | 0.65 m | Small dog |
| Adolescent | 0.90 m | 1.6 m | Near Shir's chest on all fours |
| Adult | 1.5 m | 3.6 m | Small-car-sized body; strong shoulders for later riding |

Adult tail adds approximately 2 m. Keep the adult on four feet for scale comparisons; an upright adolescent pose in the reference sheet is not its standing shoulder height. Establish a proper saddle/handhold and a consistent riding pose with the owner before those chapters.

Across all stages: light turquoise main scales, warm yellow belly, apricot wing undersides, amber eyes, two ivory horns (only rounded buds on the hatchling), golden star-shaped forehead scale, four legs, two wings, one tail. Wing backs are turquoise. Avoid new decorative marks or changing the forehead star's position.

Reference sheets guide identity and anatomy; perspective views are not exact orthographic measurements. Use a stage-specific canonical reference for each scene, never the whole growth lineup when only one age is needed.
